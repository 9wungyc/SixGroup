Asian Institute of Technology Thailand

  • AIT is an international postgraduate institution offering Master's and Doctoral programs in English.

  • It attracts students from over 50 countries, specializing in Engineering, Environment, Resources, Development, and Management.

  • AIT focuses on research in climate change, smart communities, food-energy-water systems, and sustainability, and is recognized with global awards for excellence.

Types of scholarship For Leaners

Royal Thai Govenment Scholarships

Fully funded Master's and PhD scholarships for outstanding Asian students to in Engineering,Management and Environment at AIT.

AIT Scholarship

Internal scholarships automatically considered upon application; may cover part or full tution based on academic excellence.

KAAD-AIT Scholarship

Partial fellowship(tution, monthly, stipend, housing) for Master's and Doctoral students from CLMV and Timor-Leste; includes service obligation to return home.

UMG Scholarship

Covers 24 credits and four semester's tuition / registration fees for Myanmar or Indonesian students in selected Engineering Programs, with a post-graduation job opportunity.

Required Qualifications

For Master's Degree

  • Must have already completed BE(Bachelor of Engineering) degree at University.
  • IELTS : 5.0
  • AIT English Entrance Test(EET) : over 5.0
  • GPA : 2.75

For Doctorate(Ph.D.)

  • Must have completed a Master's (ME /MSC) degree at University.

  • IELTS : 5.5
  • AIT English Entrance Test(EET) : over 5.5
  • GPA : 3.5
  • A research proposal must be submitted to the university.
