Overview

The University of Miyazaki (UOM) is a top public university in Japan, supporting international students with scholarships and focusing on innovation and collaboration in diverse fields.

Intention

The Global Scholarship supports talented Myanmar students at the University of Miyazaki, promoting excellence, cross-cultural understanding, and contributions to society through financial aid and guidance in fields like engineering, medicine, agriculture, education, and innovation.

Required Qualifications

Undergraduate Requirements

  • Completed high school (or equivalent)
  • Good academic record (Math, Science, English)
  • IELTS/TOEFL valid score report
  • 1 recommendation letter
  • Motivation statement
  • Passport or national ID (copy)

Graduate (Master’s/PhD) Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in a related field
  • Strong GPA (often 3.0/4.0+)
  • IELTS/TOEFL (higher score may be required)
  • 2–3 recommendation letters
  • Statement of Purpose
  • Research proposal (for research programs)
  • CV/Resume

Scholarship Opportunities

MEXT Scholarship

  • Undergraduate: ¥117,000 yen/month ≈ 1,667,590 MMK
  • Master: ¥144,000 yen/month ≈ 2,054,880 MMK
  • Doctor: ¥145,000 yen/month ≈ 2,070,150 MMK

Tuition Exemption Program

  • Students of UOM can apply
  • Depending on financial circumstances and academic performance
  • Students from ASEAN, Partner Universities (such as UTYCC) can apply for exchange/dual degree programs

JASSO (Japan Student Services Organization)

  • Monthly Stipend: ¥80,000
  • Eligibility: Available for students in the University of Miyazaki’s International Student Exchange Program (Inbound & Outbound)
